Our name

A field of willows, carried from Ireland to Yonkers.

Willowfield is the name of the Reilly family home in Ireland. When Mike Reilly started the company in 2011, he brought that name — and everything it stands for — to Westchester.

What began as one craftsman with three decades of experience has grown into a team of estimators, project managers and skilled tradespeople who treat every job like it's happening in their own home. High-end residential renovations, commercial interiors, and the kind of finish work that only shows up when someone actually cares.

We're small enough that the owner still walks every site, and established enough to run a NYC DOB permit, a co-op board, and a full build-out without missing a beat.

"If it wouldn't pass in my own home, it doesn't leave the site." — Mike Reilly, Founder

How we work

Consultation & Site Walk

02
